Sources: Kargoz guide (5.10) + stillsixty, The Forever Era, wow-forever.gg · updated: 17.10.2026 · numbers may change before release

  • Traps work IN COMBAT — one fire + one frost at a time (separate ~30 s cooldowns). The biggest hunter buff in Forever.
  • Summon Hawk (BM, from lvl 25) — shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot: pick hawk OR shot. In combat, keep two hawks up.
  • The pet inherits 100% of your crit and 10% of AP, and in Forever it also scales with your gear.
  • Immolation Trap = the best damage per mana; in melee: macro Raptor Strike + trap.
  • Shooting on the move: Auto Shot has only a 0.5 s standstill, after which you can move again. The longer beta pause is a bug — Blizzard is fixing it (as of 12.10). Dead zone ~8 yd — when a mob reaches you: step back or melee weave (separate swing timers).
  • Escape: Disengage (less aggro) → Feign Death (from lvl 30).
  • Mana is the limit — you can't click everything: immo > hawk ≈ serpent.
  • Dwarf: Big Game Hunter (damage bonus vs beasts) + Stoneform. Racial weapon bonuses don't work like in Classic — no need to chase a gun.
  • You can't tame animals above your level (blocked since 1.10) — take a pet at your own level.

Tricks from Robcat's intermediate PvE guide (video, 11.10)

  • Rapid Fire: use it whenever it's up — +40% attack speed (melee & ranged) for 15 s; with Rapid Killing the CD is 3 min, so it's basically always there.
  • Feign Death works by distance now: farther = reliable, close-in it almost always fails. Press it the moment a mob looks at you after you unload burst — it's your threat-wipe button (that's why tanks put up with hunters).
  • Freezing Trap counts as an interrupt — drop it in front of a caster and the cast stops (it breaks right after, but good interrupts are rare).
  • Freeze-through-Serpent trick: your Serpent Sting breaks Freezing Trap on damage — apply Scorpid Sting first (no damage, replaces Serpent), then freeze safely.
  • Ranged hit table: ranged shots can't be dodged, parried or glanced — ~90% hit chance even vs +3 mobs. That's exactly why you can kite skull-level elites.
  • Raptor-queue trick: queue Raptor Strike just before contact, then drop an Immolation Trap mid-run — the trap takes the GCD and Raptor lands free (bonus damage for nothing).
  • Spirit is worth more at 30: each tree's 2-point mana-regen talent works off it — you almost never sit to drink (or skip it and buy Sweet Nectar).
  • Distracting Shot = tank helper: a stray ranged mob out of the tank's reach — taunt it and walk it into range.
  • Aspect of the Beast = melee AP + untrackable now. Don't macro-swap aspects (toggle bugs waste mana) — keep them on separate keys; dungeons/raids: Hawk or Beast, open world: Monkey + Cheetah.
  • Summon Hawk (tested): ~158 damage per hawk with full uptime (8 swings ×13 + ~54 opener); Frenzy does not work on hawks (yet).

How to create a macro (30 s)

  • Esc → Macros → New (or type /macro) → pick an icon + name → paste the code into the big box → Save → drag the icon onto your action bar.
  • /cast = use an ability · [brackets] = conditions: [@cursor] (at the mouse), [combat], [@pet,dead], [@mouseover,harm] · #showtooltip = correct icon + tooltip.
  • A macro runs its lines top to bottom — one button can do several things. /stopmacro [condition] stops the rest if the condition is true.
  • Edit names to match what your character has (food, potions, ranks). Where a name has a rank like Mongoose Bite(Rank 4) you can drop the rank to always cast your best one.

  2. Bestial Swiftness (pet movement)
  3. Unleashed Fury — max it (flat dmg > crit; also works on hawks)

  5. Ferocity (pets and hawks)

After 40 and at 60

  • After 40 add MM (eventually ~35/16/0): Lethal Attacks → Careful Aim → Rapid Killing → Trueshot Aura.
  • MM path from 20: Hawk Eye → Lethal Attacks → Careful Aim → Rapid Killing; Lone Wolf (11 points) only for the no-pet variant.
  • At 60: raid BM 31/20/0 or MM with Lone Wolf (no pet) — respecs cost pennies.

S-tier per Kargoz: Hawk (8–12% DPS) · Bestial Swiftness · Bestial Wrath · Unleashed Fury. Flex: Imp. Revive Pet · Spirit Bond (1) · Imp. Monkey (1). Choice: Endurance Training (+pet HP/armor) vs the speed of Deadly Aspects. PvP: BM/SV variant with Entrapment. Beta respec = 1 silver — test freely.

Respec price after launch (as of 13.10): Blizzard told TechRadar that respecs will not be cheap once the game is live — the price climbs quickly the more often you respec, and only drops back once a week. There are no plans for a third spec (two specs stay the cap), and Blizzard plans weekly balance updates at first. So pick a leveling build that stays useful and don't respec for fun at launch.

How to get one and keep it

  • Lvl 10: the "Taming the Beast" quest from the hunter trainer (every starting zone has its own version) → you get Tame/Call/Dismiss/Feed/Revive Pet. Mend Pet from 12, Eyes of the Beast from 14, Beast Lore from 24.
  • You can only tame animals ≤ your level (since 1.10 taming above doesn't work — come back once you catch up).
  • Scaling: 100% of your crit / 10% AP / +2 HP per 1 stamina / 30% armor — everything also grows with your gear. Doesn't take player buffs.
  • Happiness drops (at max loyalty too!) — feed regularly, because an unhappy pet hits weaker. Meat/fish per its diet.
  • Mend Pet < 50% HP. Revive Pet = 10 s cast + all base mana — better to prevent than to fix.
  • Solo / open world — Growl stays ON: the pet is your tank (that's its job). In a dungeon (a real tank is present): Growl OFF — it's the pet's taunt: it steals the mob from the tank, so the pet dies fast, mobs ping-pong (casters lose the fight, the healer pays) and the tank fights to get aggro back. Right-click the icon or use the "Growl on/off" macro; back ON after you leave. Emergency (mob is on the healer): you can still click Growl manually once.
  • Eyes of the Beast = free scouting before a hard pull. New pet movement command (RTS-style) — easier micro.

Pet buffs — how they really work (as of 16.10)

  • Happiness = your main pet buff: green 125% dmg · yellow 100% · red 75% + losing loyalty. Feed what the family eats (cat = meat/fish), food close to the pet's level works best, not in combat. A fresh tame starts hungry — feed it right away; resting in an inn/city restores happiness.
  • Player stat buffs don't reach the pet anymore — no Fortitude / Mark of the Wild / scrolls (Forever change). The pet's "buff" is your gear: it inherits 100% of your crit, 10% of AP and keeps growing with your items.
  • Family buffs: 🐺 Wolf — Furious Howl (party melee Attack Power; now a sustained buff, −40% vs old Classic), 🦃 Tallstrider — Dust Cloud (enemy armor shred), 🦊 Fox — Trickster's Dance (pet gets +dodge & attack speed).
  • Your big pet buffs (BM): Bestial Wrath (lvl 40) +50% pet damage for 18 s; Intimidation = on-demand stun. Mend Pet heals; Feed = happiness — two different jobs!
  • Loyalty feeds training points: Rebellious → … → Best Friend. It only climbs while the pet is happy and earning XP — a death or a needless Dismiss (−50 happiness) sets you back.

Ranged weapon = absolute priority

  • Every 1 DPS of weapon ≈ +14 ranged AP — swap it for any better drop/quest reward.
  • Stopgaps (white from vendors): 6 Hunting Bow · 16 Fine Light Crossbow · 29 Heavy Crossbow · 51 Heartseeking Crossbow. Gun-only? Any higher-DPS gun does the same job.
  • Slower weapon = better for Aimed/Multi/Sniper (less auto-shot clipping).
  • 🎯 Gun upgrades for a gun-only run (as of 14.10): Skorn's Rifle from the quest "The Demon Scarred Cloak" (Skull Rock, Durotar — around lvl 8–12) and the Calibrated Blunderbuss from the quest "Old Ironforge Incursion" in The Hall of Thanes (13–18). Both are guns, so they never break your Gun skill.
  • Weapon skill grows with use — switching type (bow ↔ gun) = temporary misses, so don't hop between types.
  • 🔫 Gun-only playthrough: always carry bullets (guns don't use arrows!), keep Gun as your only ranged skill (no switching = no misses) — and the ⭐ Master Hunter's Rifle above is a gun.
  • ⭐ From 28 in STV: Hemet's quests → Master Hunter's Rifle (the best weapon of that stage).
  • Truthseeker's Bow → now requires level 40 (beta change — "sorry Hunters").

Ammo and supplies

  • Buy ammo in BULK (an empty quiver = zero damage); Leatherworking makes quivers/ammo pouches (they give faster shots).
  • Water for yourself + food for the pet on every trip.

Professions

  • Skinning + Leatherworking — the classic hunter pair (mail wearers craft their own upgrades: Hunters, Shamans, Rogues): the pet leaves a mountain of skins — armor, quiver and some BiS pieces (600+ new recipes in Forever). Start Skinning as early as you can and skin everything you and your pet kill — the leather goes straight into your own gear.
  • 🧵 Leveling gear you craft yourself (as of 14.10): with Leatherworking your level-30 target head, chest and waist are all crafted from the new rare leather recipes — Brawler's Leather Helm, Dusky Leather Armor, Stalker's Leather Belt. Early on the cheap trainer pieces carry you (Handstitched → Fine Leather Tunic, Embossed Leather Gloves/Cloak). None of them are weapons, so they never touch your Gun skill — safe for a gun-only run.
  • Skinning + Mining — the most gold (sell everything). Mining + Herbalism = double-gathering: income wherever you go.
  • Cooking — near-mandatory: food gives +5% XP from the very first mobs (small eggs, boar meat — cook batches, keep it up); eating seated (10 s) = Well Fed 15 min. The fishing vendor sells fish recipes — level cooking + fishing together.
  • First Aid — bandages work IN combat (unlike food) on you and allies — a PvP staple; Forever also lets you craft healing potions.
  • Engineering for hunters — craftable ammo + an item with extra hit chance ("great piece for hunters"); recommended for Hunter/Warrior/Rogue — utility, PvP, sells well.
  • Fishing start trick: at skill 1 use a Shiny Bauble (+25 fishing for 10 min) — the start is the hardest; cities are easiest; each catch = +1 skill.
  • Enchanting — the strongest all-round pick in Forever: enchanter-only ring enchants (+AP), selling enchants on the AH is new, all trash gear → dust/shards → gold.
  • Skinning gives +5% damage vs beasts & dragonkin (nice for grinding); Mining gives +5% max Health.

Camp system (new)

  • Sit at a camp 60 s → amenity buffs (they scale with your level). Tiers unlock at lvl 4 / 28 / 60.
  • Cooking’s campfire is the prerequisite — without one you can’t place other amenities at all (basic fire = 3 slots, journeyman = 5, expert = 10).
  • Most camp buffs don’t stack with class buffs (mining’s +90 AP vs paladin’s Might; enchanting’s t1 vs druid’s Mark; fishing’s +8% stats vs Kings).
  • Standouts: enchanting t1 ≈ 308 armor / +13 all stats / +22 all res · mining t1 up to +90 AP · First Aid +56 Stam at 60 · fishing +8% all stats.

  • ❌ Shooting in melee — there's a dead zone: step back and let the pet grab the mob.
  • ❌ Old weapon — the biggest DPS loss; always take the ranged quest reward.
  • ❌ No ammo / water / pet food before a long trip → forced downtime.
  • ❌ Taming above your level — doesn't work (since 1.10).
  • ❌ Lone Wolf with a pet out = a wasted talent (+20% works only WITHOUT a pet).
  • ❌ Forgetting to feed — an unhappy pet = less damage.
  • ❌ Growl left ON in a dungeon — your pet taunts the tank's mob: dead pet, ping-ponging pack, an angry healer. Toggle it off at the entrance (one macro press).
  • ❌ Cheetah in combat — any damage gives 4 s daze; Cheetah is for travel only.
  • ❌ Feign Death left on for 6+ min without canceling = you really die 😅 — get up right after the reset.
  • ✅ Hunter's Mark works at 100 yd and lasts 2 min — mark the next mob while still fighting.
  • ✅ Well Fed — eat seated before a hard stretch (10 s → 15 min buff).
  • ✅ Buy ammo/water in bulk before each dungeon (the campsite has vendors too).
  • ✅ Mend the pet before it's a drama; Eyes of the Beast for scouting; bandage when mana is zero.
  • ❌ Downranking shots to save mana — much weaker since Oct 1 (low ranks lose spell power and rarely trigger talents). Use your top rank (as of 14.10).
  • ⚠️ Gold buying is policed (as of 14.10): a suspect account can get a Temporary Economy Hold — trade, mail, guild bank and AH blocked until Blizzard checks. Buy gold from a third party and your whole account is at risk.
